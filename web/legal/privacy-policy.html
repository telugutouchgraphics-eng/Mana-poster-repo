Data Protection

Privacy Policy

This page explains in detail how Mana Poster Ai collects, stores, uses, shares, and protects account data, poster profile data, religion preference, notifications, subscriptions, ads, moderation records, and Firebase-powered app services.

Last updated: May 23, 2026

What We Collect

We may collect and process your email address, display name, Firebase UID, Google Sign-In information, profile photo, poster profile details, personal or business branding details, logo image, WhatsApp number, notification token, app language preference, religion preference selected inside the app, subscription entitlement status, billing verification metadata, purchase tokens, referral code details, referral attribution details, uploaded poster assets, moderation status, support messages, device-level app preference settings, and limited technical diagnostics required to operate the app.

Some information is provided directly by you, such as your login identity, poster profile, religion selection, language selection, uploaded content, and notification settings. Some information is generated automatically by the app or backend, such as Firebase identifiers, purchase verification records, notification delivery metadata, crash diagnostics, and review history for uploaded posters.

How We Use Data

We use collected information to provide login and authentication, secure your account, save your app choices, personalize poster generation and poster categories, support save and export flows, deliver push notifications, verify and restore purchases, manage referrals, reduce abuse, moderate uploaded content, investigate complaints, maintain platform safety, comply with platform obligations, and provide customer support.

We also use selected preferences, such as language and religion, to shape the in-app experience. For example, the app may show or hide certain category groups based on the religion option chosen by the user. This is intended to personalize the content feed and simplify browsing. It is not intended to make legal, financial, medical, or identity decisions about a user.

Religion Preference and Sensitive Preference Handling

Mana Poster Ai may ask a signed-in user to choose a religion preference such as Hindu, Muslim, Christian, or All. We treat this field as a personalization preference used only inside the app experience. We recognize that religion can be considered sensitive personal information in many privacy frameworks, so we limit its use to a narrow purpose.

The religion preference is used to control which category groups are emphasized, shown, hidden, or filtered in parts of the app, especially the home feed and category browsing experience. In simple terms, this preference is used to personalize which poster categories appear most relevant for the user inside the app.

We do not request this preference to identify your legal status, caste, ethnicity, citizenship, health, financial status, or eligibility for any service. We do not use this preference to make automated eligibility decisions. We do not use it for credit, lending, insurance, employment, housing, or similar profiling.

We do not sell your religion preference. We do not publish it publicly in the app. Other ordinary users are not shown your religion choice. We do not display it on your public poster profile. It is not meant to be a public-facing profile field.

Where technically required for app continuity, your religion preference may be stored locally on your device and may also be synced to your signed-in user record in our backend so that the app can preserve your selection across app sessions. If you reinstall the app, the onboarding flow may ask you to choose again on that device even if an older preference exists on the backend, depending on the current app flow design. This is done to make sure the in-app experience is intentionally confirmed by the user on that device.

You can change your religion preference later from inside app settings. Because this preference is meant for personalization, we recommend that users choose the option that best matches the category experience they want to see inside the app. If you do not want religion-based filtering, you may choose the "All" option where available.

How Religion Preference Is Stored and Used

When available, religion preference may be stored in two places: a device-local app setting and a backend account record linked to your signed-in user ID. Device-local storage helps the app remember the preference on that particular install. Backend storage can help preserve continuity across sessions and support product logic for signed-in users.

We use the religion preference only for app-side content presentation, filtering, onboarding continuity, and related preference management. We do not intentionally use it for third-party advertising segmentation, cross-app behavioral targeting, or sale to data brokers.

If analytics, crash reporting, or diagnostic systems capture app state indirectly, those systems may process operational events around app screens or settings interactions. However, our intent is that the religion preference itself remains a product preference used narrowly for content personalization and settings persistence rather than a broadly shared advertising field.

Community Uploads and Review

Users may upload poster images for manager review. We may process the uploaded image, selected category, upload time, applicable visibility date, review status, rejection reason, contribution share/download counts, and related moderation history. Approved uploads may become visible to other users in the related category. Managers and admins may review, reject, remove, or retain pending, rejected, or policy-violating uploads as part of moderation and record-keeping.

Firebase, Analytics, and Ads

The app uses Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Storage, Messaging, Analytics, Crashlytics, Google Sign-In, Google Play Billing, and AdMob. These services support app performance, crash diagnostics, notifications, billing verification, content retrieval, and ad delivery. AdMob and related providers may collect device identifiers, approximate location derived from IP, app interaction data, and advertising signals to support personalized or non-personalized ads depending on settings, consent state, platform behavior, and applicable law.

Our intention is not to use religion preference as an advertising audience field. Religion preference is collected for in-app category personalization. However, if you use advertising-supported parts of the app, third-party ad systems may still process general device and usage data under their own privacy terms. Those ad systems should be reviewed separately if you want more information about ad personalization technologies.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data, including your religion preference. Data may be shared only with service providers that help us operate the app, such as authentication, cloud hosting, storage, crash reporting, notifications, subscription verification, and advertising partners where applicable. Data may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary for billing support, content moderation, fraud prevention, security investigations, legal compliance, law enforcement requests, or protection of users and the platform.

We aim to restrict sharing to the minimum required for the relevant task. Internal access to user data should also be limited to people, systems, or workflows that need the information for support, moderation, account operations, or service reliability.

Permissions are requested only for photo selection, poster saving, and optional notifications. You can manage them from device settings. You remain responsible for any content you upload, export, or share. The app may temporarily cache media files and remove them when they are no longer needed.

Your Choices and Account Deletion

After deletion, login access, poster profile details, linked app data, and personalization records may be deleted or scheduled for deletion from active systems. This may include religion preference data associated with your account, subject to backup cycles, fraud prevention obligations, billing record retention, legal compliance, or platform-required retention windows. More details: Account Deletion.

Retention of Preference Data

We keep app preference data, including religion preference, only for as long as it is reasonably needed to operate your account, preserve app continuity, support the user experience, or meet legal and operational obligations. If you change your religion preference, the newer value may replace the older one in active systems, though earlier values may remain temporarily in logs, backups, or operational history for a limited period.

If you delete your account, we will aim to remove or anonymize active account-linked preference data within our normal deletion processes, except where a limited retention period is necessary for fraud prevention, abuse investigations, dispute handling, tax, billing, legal compliance, or technical backup recovery windows.
