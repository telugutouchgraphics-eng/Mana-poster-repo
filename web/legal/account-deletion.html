Account Deletion

Mana Poster Ai provides an in-app account deletion request option. Users can request complete data deletion from inside the app or by contacting support.

What to include in the request

  • Registered email address or phone number
  • Any active subscription context if support is needed before deletion
  • Confirmation that you want permanent account deletion

What Happens After Deletion

  • Login access may be removed.
  • Poster profile details and linked app data may be removed.
  • Some billing, tax, anti-fraud, or platform-required records may be retained for a limited period.
